# Child-facing Arabic for ages 4–6

- Modern Standard Arabic, simple and spoken-friendly; vowel any word the narrator could misread.
- Sentences of 6–10 words; one idea per sentence; concrete nouns the child can see in the picture.
- Address the child warmly in the second person; prefer gender-neutral phrasing, or provide both forms as two records if unavoidable.
- Western numerals (0–9) in any UI number; stored Qur'anic text is never altered.
- Praise the effort and the observation, not the child's intelligence (praise looking carefully, not being clever).
- Never: "wrong", "no", threats, fear of punishment, comparing children, sarcasm.
- Name Allah's action plainly and reverently (Allah sends the rain; Allah makes the seed grow); keep abstract creed terms out of level A–B lines.
- Every hint is shorter than the question it supports.
- Narration cards: 2–5 words each, paired with a picture, so a child can "tell it back" without reading.
- Prefer verbs the child can act out: look, listen, count, put in order.
