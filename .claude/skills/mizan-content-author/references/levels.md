# Content levels (Reference Package p.2; Runbook v1.2 §4.2)

Most restrictive level wins. When unsure between two levels, choose the stricter one.

| Level | Package scope | MIZAN behaviour for a 4–6-year-old | Example child question |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — stable, foundational | Qur'an, approved authentic hadith, pillars of Islam and iman, basic seerah, ethics and values, stable introductory information | Direct answer at child level from the approved library; source shown on screen and in the parent summary | من أنزل المطر؟ |
| B — explanation and reasoning | Explaining concepts, comparisons, purposes of the law, general intellectual questions | Answer from approved material with its reference; no definitive wording where scholars differ | لماذا تحتاج النبتة إلى الماء؟ |
| C — disputed or highly sensitive | Fiqh disagreement, detailed creed, contested history, questions needing specialist research | Reviewer-written approved answer if one exists; otherwise gentle referral to the parents | أين يذهب الإنسان إذا مات؟ |
| D — personal ruling | Ruling on an individual case, validity of a person's worship or contract, family dispute, legal or medical matters with a sharia effect | No ruling; brief general information if appropriate; referral to a parent or qualified scholar | هل صلاة أبي صحيحة إذا…؟ |
| OUT_OF_SCOPE — adult questions | Outside the children's journey | Calm scope statement, no debate, referral | هل انتشر الإسلام بالسيف؟ |

## Defaults for this journey

- Death, the afterlife, the unseen (angels, jinn, details of Paradise), Allah's attributes beyond "Allah created / sends / makes grow": **C**.
- Anything about a specific person's worship, family situation or permission to do something: **D**.
- Questions a 4–6-year-old would not ask but a judge might type (history, polemics, comparative religion): **OUT_OF_SCOPE**.
- A child disclosing harm or danger: not a content level — use the safety referral record (CLAUDE.md §5.2).

## Tone

The child must not be able to tell that a rule fired. Referral lines stay warm and curious: the question was a good one, and a parent is the best person to answer it together.
